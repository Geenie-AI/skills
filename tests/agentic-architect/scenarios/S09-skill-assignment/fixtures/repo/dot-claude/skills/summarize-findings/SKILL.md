---
name: summarize-findings
description: Use when asked to summarize research findings into a handoff summary of established, contested and unknown points.
---

1. List every fact in the findings with its citation.
2. Group into established (two or more independent sources), contested (sources disagree), unknown (no source).
3. Write one paragraph per group, each sentence ending with its citation.
Done when every fact in the input appears in exactly one group.

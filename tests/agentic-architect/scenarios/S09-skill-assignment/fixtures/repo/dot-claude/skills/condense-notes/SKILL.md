---
name: condense-notes
description: Use when research notes or findings are too long to work with and you need to condense them without losing sourced claims.
---

1. Remove duplicated claims, keeping the one with the stronger source.
2. Merge claims that say the same thing from different sources, listing all sources.
3. Drop unsourced commentary.
Done when the text is at most half its original length and every remaining claim has a source.

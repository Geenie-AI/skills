---
name: review-checklist
description: Use when reviewing a brief before it goes to a client; the house rule list for claims, format, tone, length and client naming.
---

Check in order: every claim cited and supported; house structure (headline, three findings, implication, sources); tone neutral; length under two pages; client named exactly as in the engagement record; no competitor named without a citation; dates absolute.
Done when each item is marked pass or has a numbered fix.
